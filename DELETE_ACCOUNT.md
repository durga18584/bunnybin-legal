# Delete Your BunnyBin Account and Data

BunnyBin is developed by **[YOUR DEVELOPER NAME AS SHOWN ON PLAY STORE]**.
This page explains how to request deletion of your account and the data
associated with it.

## How to request deletion

**Option 1 — In the app (fastest):**
1. Open the BunnyBin app and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Type `DELETE` to confirm.
5. Tap **Delete my account**.

Your account is deleted immediately — no waiting period.

**Option 2 — If you no longer have access to the app:**
Email **durgabizi07@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your
account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We'll verify
your identity and process the deletion within 7 days.

## What gets deleted

- Your profile information (email, display name, profile photo link)
- Your subscription/entitlement record

## What we retain, and for how long

- **Transaction and billing records** are kept even after account
  deletion, for up to **7 years**, as required for financial recordkeeping.
  This data is not linked to your deleted profile for any purpose beyond
  that recordkeeping requirement.
- **Data on your device** (scanned files, tags, and scheduled-deletion
  records) lives entirely locally on your device and is never sent to us
  in the first place — account deletion doesn't touch it. To clear it
  separately, use Settings → **Erase local tagging data**, or simply
  uninstall the app.
