# BunnyBin Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 13, 2026

This policy explains what information BunnyBin ("the app", "we", "us")
collects, why, and what never leaves your device. BunnyBin is a personal
file-tagging and scheduled-deletion app for Android (and, in future, iOS).

## The short version

BunnyBin never sees the contents of your photos or videos. Scanning,
tagging, and deleting files all happen entirely on your device. The only
data that reaches our servers is what's needed to run your account and
subscription — nothing about your files themselves.

## Information we collect

**Account information**, via Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In or
email/password):
- Email address
- Display name and profile photo URL, if you sign in with Google or set one
- A unique account identifier

**Subscription and purchase information**, via Google Play Billing:
- Which plan you're on and its status (active, expired, cancelled)
- A record of purchase transactions, used for support and billing history

We never receive your payment card details or other payment credentials —
Google Play processes all payments directly, and only tells us whether a
purchase succeeded and which plan it was for.

## Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store:
- The contents of your photos, videos, or any other files
- Thumbnails, previews, or any data derived from your files' content
- File names, folder paths, or any other file metadata

All file scanning, tagging decisions, and scheduled-deletion logic run
locally on your device, in a local on-device database. None of it is ever
sent to us. This is a deliberate design choice, not an oversight — it's the
reason the app can work fully offline for everything except signing in and
managing your subscription.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Photos/media access** — to scan your device's media library for files
  to tag. This scan runs entirely on-device; nothing is uploaded.
- **Notifications** — to remind you about files scheduled for deletion
  before they're removed.

## Third-party services

BunnyBin uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own
privacy policy:

- **Firebase Authentication** (Google) — handles sign-in; see
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google Play Billing** (Google) — handles subscription purchases.
- **Google AdMob** (Google) — shows ads to users on the free plan; may
  collect advertising identifiers for ad delivery and measurement, subject
  to Google's own policies.

## Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Doing so removes
your profile and subscription data. We retain past transaction/billing
records as required for financial recordkeeping, even after account
deletion.

## Children's privacy

BunnyBin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from them.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of
transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute
security.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be
reflected by updating the effective date above.
