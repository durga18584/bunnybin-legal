## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does BunnyBin ever see my photos or videos?**
No. All scanning, tagging, and deletion happen entirely on your device. Nothing about your files — no images, videos, thumbnails, or extracted text — is ever uploaded to or stored on our servers.

**What information does BunnyBin collect?**
Only what's needed to run your account: your email/name for sign-in, and basic subscription and app-usage records. See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for full details.

**How do I delete my account and data?**
See [Account Deletion](DELETE_ACCOUNT.md), or go to Settings → Account → Delete Account inside the app.

**I tagged a file and it got deleted — can I get it back?**
Once a file is actually deleted from your device — after its scheduled grace period and a final confirmation step — BunnyBin can't recover it, since deletion happens through your device's own operating system, not through us. If you want to keep a file, restore it from the Upcoming Deletions screen before its deletion date.

**I'm having trouble with a purchase or subscription.**
Email **durgabizi07@gmail.com** with the email address on your account and we'll help sort it out. For refunds on App Store purchases specifically, these are handled directly by Apple, not by us — use Apple's [Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) page.

**How do I reach you for anything else?**
Email **durgabizi07@gmail.com** — we read every message.
